Dear Point Hiring Team,

I am applying for the Senior Software Engineer position on the Docket Operations
team. It sits exactly where my recent work has been: building the systems and
integrations an operations team depends on, and putting AI to work inside them
rather than alongside them. The product is also one I find worth building for,
since it lets homeowners access equity they already hold without another monthly
payment, and the operations behind that have to be both fast and careful.

I am a Senior Software Engineer at Numa, working on an AI phone assistant for car
dealerships in production. I build Gemini and GPT into the live call flow, where
latency, failure handling and keeping the agent within safe boundaries matter
more than raw model capability. I also build and maintain the dealer management
system integrations beneath it, having led the Reynolds \& Reynolds one and helped
build CDK Global, so the assistant can book against live dealership records. My
own development is AI-assisted, in Cursor and Claude Code.
Production AI, integrations with systems we do not control, and AI-accelerated
delivery are three things your posting asks for, and what I do now.

Before Numa I spent three years at Divisions Maintenance Group, building the
backend services behind a platform that connects maintenance providers with work
across the United States. Earlier roles took me through electric vehicle charging
at AddEnergie, maritime compliance software at Lloyd's Register OneOcean, and a
financial policy and insurance platform at Stratec Global. The work was much the
same across all of them: designing distributed backend systems, keeping them
maintainable as they grew, and improving how the team shipped them. My MSc in
Computer Science is from UQAM, where my research on architectural patterns in
Android applications was published at SAC'19.

What draws me to this team is that Docket Operations sits close to the people
doing the work, where an engineering decision shows up in someone's day rather
than several quarters later. I would contribute on that basis: owning features
end to end, writing designs others can review properly, and supporting teammates
through code review and onboarding. Having worked remotely from Montréal with
United States teams since 2022, I am settled in the way Point operates, and would
welcome the chance to bring that to a company at this stage of its growth.

Thank you for considering my application. I would be glad to discuss how I can
contribute to the Docket Operations team.
